# Analytical contribution gate

Use when drafting a complete revision or reviewing material accumulation, weak synthesis, or filler. Apply the gate to the smallest coherent **paragraph group or section**, not to each paragraph independently.

## Contribution in context

For an analytical unit, identify the relevant facts, its finding, the reasoning connecting them, and the resulting interpretation or decision. These may be distributed across several paragraphs. A fact table, definition, example, calculation, or transition can contribute by supporting the unit's argument; do not append a forced implication to each one.

Assess the unit according to its declared purpose. An analytical section needs synthesis, but a methodology note may explain a calculation and an appendix may document sources. Neither needs an invented strategic conclusion. Ask “What does this unit help the intended reader understand or decide?” rather than “Does every paragraph state a new insight?”

Investigate units dominated by cautions without resolution, generic advice without a diagnosis, lists whose significance remains unexplained, or risks without a recommendation verdict. These are contextual diagnostics, not deletion triggers. A legitimate limitation can be the substantive finding. Repair supported reasoning or recommend the appropriate change under `SKILL.md`'s authority gates. Do not invent a finding to keep a section alive or silently remove it when no useful repair is available.

## Replacement filler

Weak source: “A policy will bring exposure, talent, resources, and valuation growth.”

Weak revision: “The policy creates a positive environment but does not guarantee growth, which still depends on execution.”

The revision avoids overclaiming but does not resolve the claimed effects. Determine what the policy actually changes for this report's question. The result could be a concrete opportunity, a market-wide change in expectations, a constraint, a useful brief contextual fact, or a proposal to remove irrelevant material. Do not force one outcome across reports.

Weak source: “The product is technologically ahead of all competitors.”

Weak revision: “Technical leadership cannot be proven, so comparison should focus on experience.”

Apply a defensible comparison basis and state the actual result. It may show advantage, parity, disadvantage, conditional advantage, or a specific unresolved dimension. A substantive conclusion need not be a favorable verdict; do not manufacture superiority or differentiation.

## Conditional section guidance

### Competitors

For competitive positioning, compare named relevant alternatives using shared decision-relevant dimensions. Separate product, supplier, and infrastructure levels when they answer different questions. State similarities, differences, and the customers or conditions affected. If the information supports parity or disadvantage, say so. If one dimension remains indeterminate, name it precisely and state what can be concluded on the others. Do not demand impossible comparability or merely instruct the author to “compare again.”

### Policy

Match the mechanism to the claim. A direct subsidy, approval benefit, or operating constraint needs the instrument, eligibility, and connection to the actor/activity. A macro or industry report may legitimately analyze indirect effects on incentives, expectations, financing, or legitimacy without attributing a measurable near-term effect to one company. Support the mechanism at an appropriate level of certainty; do not require every policy paragraph to end in a product KPI. Keep necessary context concise. Propose removal or relocation only when irrelevant to the report's purpose, with applicable authorization.

### Marketing strategy and execution (营销策略与执行)

UGC (用户创作内容) is one possible form of content and participation within marketing, not a parallel category to marketing itself. Review it where relevant to the report; do not impose a separate UGC section or use “营销与UGC” as a default heading.

Judge actions against objective, audience behavior, assets, channel fit, constraints, and competing priorities. For differentiated campaigns, explain the product-specific reason to watch, discuss, try, or share. For an operational plan, common practices such as asset preparation, permission rules, creator outreach, and measurement can be essential if they address the diagnosed bottleneck. Actions need not be unique to be useful. Generic campaign hygiene is inadequate as the entire strategic insight, but is not disposable merely because other companies use it. Do not invent a replacement campaign outside the existing scope.

### Users and demand

Connect observed behavior, product characteristics, or testing responses to a defensible need or tradeoff. A persona may be a grounded inference without primary survey data. Avoid unsupported demographic or psychological traits; distinguish the supported need from a speculative extension. Recommend specific corrections instead of labeling the whole persona “待验证.”

### Technology

Match technical depth to the report's purpose. A strategy section should explain how supported capabilities affect experience, constraints, cost, scalability, or defensibility. Technical reports may need definitions and architecture descriptions as foundations. Attribute unverified company statements and do not insert fashionable components without support. Correcting an architecture is distinct from permission to replace the strategic thesis.

## Exit test

For analytical sections, ask whether the conclusion follows from support, explains more than a restatement, and helps the intended reader interpret or choose. For supporting material, ask whether its role and connection are necessary and clear. Preserve units that already work; do not manufacture another insight, recommendation, or edit to satisfy a quota.

Inspect suspicious cautions or generic wording together with surrounding paragraphs. Keep necessary subject-matter limitations; move reviewer instructions and change rationales to the review surface. A failed unit calls for a professional diagnosis and authorized repair, not automatic deletion or a compulsory research-plan substitute.
